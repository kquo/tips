## Confederate Monuments

[Confederate monuments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_monuments_and_memorials) were mostly raised decades after the Civil War, many during the Jim Crow era, and they present the Confederacy through the [Lost Cause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_Cause_of_the_Confederacy) narrative rather than through the historical record of [slavery in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavery_in_the_United_States).

### My take

These monuments and flags memorialize a myth, and the myth helps people forget the larger context and ugliness of slavery rather than remember it. As the documentarian [Ken Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Burns) has put it, they represent stories, not facts. Myths we should not cherish.
