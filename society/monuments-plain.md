## Confederate Monuments (Plain English)

[Confederate monuments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederate_monuments_and_memorials) were mostly raised decades after the Civil War, many during the Jim Crow era. They present the Confederacy through the [Lost Cause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_Cause_of_the_Confederacy) story rather than through the historical record of [slavery in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavery_in_the_United_States). These monuments and flags honor a myth. The myth helps people forget the larger context and ugliness of slavery rather than remember it. As the documentary maker [Ken Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Burns) has put it, they represent stories, not facts. They are myths I do not think we should cherish.
