## The Internet Monetization Model

Most internet content businesses turn attention into revenue through one funnel: traffic, attention, trust, conversion, retention, expansion. It is a modern synthesis of the [purchase funnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchase_funnel), the [conversion funnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion_funnel), [AIDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDA_(marketing)), and [content marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_marketing), and it applies across creators, software subscriptions, and e-commerce.

**Traffic** is acquired as cheaply as possible from social platforms, search, and communities: high volume, low intent, algorithm-driven. **Attention** keeps people consuming through feeds, short video, and infinite scroll; platforms optimize this layer hardest because it is the prerequisite for trust. **Trust** turns anonymous users into an audience through personality, direct interaction, and consistent value; switching costs form here, psychological rather than technical. **Conversion** is the monetization event, a subscription, a purchase, or a free-to-paid upgrade, typically at one to five percent, a high-leverage point but not the main profit driver. **Retention** drives [lifetime value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer_lifetime_value) through recurring billing and habit, and decides profitability. **Expansion** raises revenue per customer through upsells, cross-sells, and high-ticket offers.

Two implementations dominate. Audience-first builds the audience and monetizes later: high margin, with the risk that monetization never comes. Product-first builds the product and buys users: predictable, with high acquisition cost. Ad-funded platforms monetize attention directly and skip conversion, marketplaces depend on liquidity more than on funnels, and search-driven businesses compress the funnel because intent already exists.

The initial sale is not where value is created. Profit comes from retention and expansion, and the rest of the funnel exists to feed them.

### Recommendation systems

The attention layer is run by [recommender systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recommender_system) tuned to keep you on the platform, because time on platform is what gets sold to advertisers. The engineers are not villains; they are answering a business incentive, and video feeds, social networks, and 24-hour cable news all run the same model. The political cost is that the tuning rewards whatever is vivid and emotionally activating, which is how [Pizzagate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizzagate_conspiracy_theory) spread. How that shapes belief is the subject of [perception management](perception-management.md); what it does to political identity is in [identity politics](us-identity-politics.md).
