## Edge Website (Plain English)

It looks silly when people brag about how much they know. Showing off knowledge makes humans look like street performers. But when people who know a lot get together and talk casually about what we know, that is a fine thing. That is what [Edge](https://www.edge.org) was: the smartest, most insightful salon on the web, and I still think it is worth a visit now and then.

As of 2023 the site had gone quiet.

The trouble is its founder. [John Brockman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Brockman_(literary_agent)) was closely tied to Jeffrey Epstein, the convicted sex offender. That does not erase the conversations. But it changes how I read the guest list.
