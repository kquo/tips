## Optimism

Nothing spreads like pessimism, and it has more to do with the mind than with the circumstances you are passing through. Two people can face the same difficulties, and according to their attitude they will meet them differently, and one will get past them faster than the other. Lines I keep for that:

- "Optimism is the faith that leads to achievement. Nothing can be done without hope and confidence." — [Helen Keller](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Helen_Keller)
- "The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do." — Apple's Think Different campaign, often credited to [Steve Jobs](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Steve_Jobs)
- "If opportunity doesn't knock, build a door." — [Milton Berle](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Milton_Berle)
- "I am an optimist. It does not seem too much use being anything else." — Winston Churchill, attributed
- "Perpetual optimism is a force multiplier." — Colin Powell, attributed
- "It's wonderful to be optimistic. It keeps you healthy and it keeps you resilient." — Daniel Kahneman, attributed
- "You'll never find a rainbow if you're looking down." — Charlie Chaplin, attributed
- "Impossible is a word to be found only in the dictionary of fools." — Napoleon Bonaparte, attributed

And some [Spanish proverbs](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Spanish_proverbs), which say it shorter:

- "Chispa pequeña enciende un monte de leña." A small spark sets a whole hillside of firewood alight.
- "Cuando una puerta se cierra, una ventana se abre." When a door closes, a window opens.
- "Quien no se arriesga, no gana." Who does not risk does not win.
- "A grandes males, grandes remedios." Great ills call for great remedies.
- "A mal tiempo, buena cara." Bad weather, good face.
