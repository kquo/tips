## Detecting Baloney

Baloney is foolish, nonsensical, or absurd words and ideas, and detecting it belongs in everyone's intellectual self-defense toolkit. My pointers:

- Read and listen to the late [Carl Sagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Sagan) and to [Michael Shermer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shermer).
- Start with Sagan's baloney detection kit from [The Demon-Haunted World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Demon-Haunted_World), summarized at [The Mind Collection](https://themindcollection.com/baloney-detection-kit/).
- Two good clips: [Shermer, clip one](https://youtu.be/mn7jVTGjb-I) and [Shermer, clip two](https://youtu.be/aNSHZG9blQQ).
- Be careful with [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=baloney+detection+kit) and social media algorithms in general; depending on the topic they are emotionally loaded.
- Build your own list of trusted sources instead of relying on whatever the algorithm serves.
- Cross-check claims with several credible outlets before accepting them.
