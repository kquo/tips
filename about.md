# About

`Bits` is a personal reference site for short, atomic notes.

It exists primarily as a place to keep short notes, reminders, and working views organized for easy reuse. Some entries are practical, some are reflective, and some are argumentative. All of them are snapshots of current thinking rather than final declarations. Expect concise notes over exhaustive treatment, clear opinions when opinions are being stated, links to general-reference sources, often Wikipedia, when they help orient the reader, and revisions over time when a view is sharpened, corrected, or replaced. Accuracy matters, but certainty is limited; the goal is to be honest, reasoned, and open to criticism and revision when better arguments or evidence appear.

The site is organized into four areas:

- [Life](life/index.md)
- [Mind](mind/index.md)
- [Society](society/index.md)
- [Tech](tech/index.md)
