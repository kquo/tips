# About

This site is how I see the world, in short entries. Some are practical how-tos, most are reflective takes, and all are snapshots of current thinking rather than final declarations. Expect brevity over exhaustive treatment, clear opinions, links to general references such as Wikipedia, and revisions whenever a view is sharpened, corrected, or replaced.

Most reflective entries also come in a Plain English version, linked at the top of the page.

To dispute a claim or request a correction, [open an issue](https://github.com/queone/bits/issues). A disputed claim gets fixed or cited in the next release.

The four areas: [Life](life/index.md), [Mind](mind/index.md), [Society](society/index.md), and [Tech](tech/index.md).
