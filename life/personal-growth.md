## Personal Growth

What I have settled on for [personal growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_development) fits in a paragraph. Keep evolving; the point is to be a different and better person than last decade's version, not to defend that version. Start from the fact that neither the universe nor anyone in it owes me anything, which turns every good thing into something to notice rather than expect. Good things do tend to happen to the lucky, but luck is partly engineered: I can put myself in the situations, rooms, and habits where it is more likely to find me. Value the small things without becoming obsessed with them, and never take them for granted. And outsource wisely: when a task sits outside my expertise or my hours, and I can afford it, paying someone who does it well is not laziness but leverage, and the older I get the more that trade is worth.
